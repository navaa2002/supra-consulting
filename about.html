Who We Are

Engineers and surveyors who measure twice, and stand behind it.

Founded in 2009, Supra Consulting has grown from a two-person survey practice into a full-service engineering and professional consultancy trusted by developers, government agencies and asset owners across Sri Lanka.

FOUNDED 2009 · COLOMBO
Our Story

Sixteen years of getting the numbers right.

Supra Consulting began with a single robotic total station and a conviction that engineering decisions should be built on verified field data, not estimates. That principle still governs every engagement we take on today.

We now operate three divisions — Survey, Engineering Consulting, and Inspections & Project Management — supported by an in-house drafting and GIS team. Our client list spans private developers, state infrastructure agencies, ports authorities and heritage conservation bodies.

Every report that leaves our office carries a chartered engineer's or licensed surveyor's signature. It's a small detail that has kept our client retention above 95% for the last five years.
